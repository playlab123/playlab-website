Ananya’s research brings together human factors, human-machine interaction design, engineering education, and psychology. She explores how augmented reality, virtual reality, robotics, and artificial intelligence can enhance human capabilities and expertise. Her work combines user-centered design, interface prototyping, qualitative and quantitative evaluation, and research on learning and skill development.
