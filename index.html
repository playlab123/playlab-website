PlayLab

Designing interactive experiences that help people learn, create, and explore.

We explore how emerging technologies can expand human capabilities and make learning more accessible. Our work brings together human-computer interaction, augmented and virtual reality, design, and learning sciences.

From practicing hands-on skills in virtual environments to bringing stories to life with augmented reality, we build and study tools that connect the physical and digital worlds. We put people at the center of the design process, exploring how interactive systems can support expertise, creativity, and everyday experiences.
