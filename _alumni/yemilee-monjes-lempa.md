Yemilee is a Chicago-based aspiring UX designer and digital communications strategist. She brings a community-centered approach to design, combining technical skill with a commitment to accessibility, advocacy, and inclusive digital experiences. Her work sits at the intersection of UX research, digital engagement, accessibility, and community empowerment. Her professional and community-based experience has strengthened her ability to communicate across diverse audiences and design with real communities in mind.
