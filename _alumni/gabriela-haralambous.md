Gabriela Haralambous is an undergraduate student at the University of Michigan-Flint pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Engineering in Mechanical Engineering. As a participant in the College of Innovation and Technology Student Undergraduate Research Experience (CIT-SURE), she developed a virtual reality escape room environment in Unity and collaborated with her team to integrate it with Arduino-based IoT sensor modules and a sensor-carrying rover. Her interests include computer-aided design, hardware-software integration, and hands-on product development.
