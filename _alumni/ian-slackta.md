Through the Summer Undergraduate Research Experience (SURE), he built the sensor backbone of a virtual reality escape room on Meta Quest 3: multi-sensor ESP32 stations streaming data over MQTT into Unity, and a library of sensor-driven puzzles across six themed rooms. His interests include embedded systems, IoT security, and HCI.
